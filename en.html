Services & Expertise

What We Offer

Beyond academic research, the group provides analytical and advisory services to public institutions, regional governments, and policy bodies.

[01]

Macroeconomic Scenario Forecasting

Projections for public budget planning in institutions, combining structural models with empirical calibration to regional and national contexts.

[02]

Welfare & Distributional Analysis of Health and Social Policies

Quantitative assessment of the distributional and welfare effects of health care reform, social services, and natality support programmes.

[03]

Impact Assessment of Productivity-Enhancing Policies

Analysis of how R&D support, innovation incentives, and investment promotion translate into aggregate productivity and long-run growth.

[04]

Effects of Financial Regulation on Business Investment

Study of how financial regulation affects firm investment decisions and macroeconomic outcomes, drawing on models of financial frictions and heterogeneous firms.

[05]

Policy Design Advisory

Evidence-based advice on natality policies, social service reform, and R&D promotion, integrating welfare-theoretic analysis with institutional context.
